## Project Overview

## Main Calculations

### Battery Energy

Battery Energy = Voltage × Capacity

48 V × 30 Ah = **1,440 Wh = 1.44 kWh**

### Usable Energy

1,440 × 0.90 = **1,296 Wh**

### Estimated Range

Range = Usable Energy / Energy Consumption

1,296 Wh / 20 Wh/km = **64.8 km**

Therefore, the estimated mixed-use range is approximately **60–65 km per charge**.
