## Battery Charging Calculation
Selected battery capacity: **60 kWh**

Selected charger power: **7.2 kW**

Ideal charging time:

**60 ÷ 7.2 = 8.33 hours**

Assuming 90% charging efficiency:

**60 ÷ (7.2 × 0.90) = 9.26 hours**

The actual charging time may vary depending on battery condition, temperature, state of charge, charger limitations, and charging control.
